# Altera — Mental Well-being App

**Altera** is a mobile application designed to help users navigate apathy and emotional burnout. Our goal is to provide a safe, engaging space for users to rediscover their energy and motivation.

## 🎯 The Problem & Our Solution

In today's fast-paced world, many people struggle with apathy and "social battery" exhaustion, often leading to isolation.

**Our Solution:**
* **Supportive Platform:** A clean, minimalist interface focused on user comfort.
* **Quest Engine:** Personalized daily mini-quests and location-based challenges to encourage positive action both at home and outdoors.
* **Anonymous Community:** A safe space for interaction where achievements are celebrated with positive reinforcement, free from toxic pressure.

## 🚀 Key Features 

* **Diagnostic & Support Tools:** State tracking and the "Future Capsule" feature.
* **Quest Engine:** Intelligent algorithms that generate tailored challenges based on user needs.
* **Social Validation:** A secure architecture for an anonymous feed designed for community support.
* **Personal Progress:** Visual tracking through photo albums to capture memories and personal growth.

## ⚙️ Development Methodology

We follow a **Hybrid-Agile** approach to balance flexibility and productivity:
* **Modified Kanban:** Continuous flow management with WIP limits and weekly planning.
* **Workflow:** Backlog → To Do → In Progress (Cross-Code Review) → QA Validation → Done.
* **Communication:** Optimized for efficiency with a focus on board transparency.
## 🚧 Future Roadmap & Development

We are actively working on expanding Altera to make it more inclusive, secure, and reliable. Our current development and testing efforts are focused on the following features:

* **Content Moderation:** Implementing an automated AI-based system to monitor and filter uploaded photos, ensuring a safe and non-toxic environment for all users.
* **Accessibility (a11y):** Making the application fully accessible to people with disabilities, including screen reader support, high-contrast modes, and optimized navigation.
* **Offline Mode:** Developing robust offline functionality, allowing users to track their mood and access essential resources even without an active internet connection.

We are committed to continuous improvement and look forward to implementing these features in upcoming updates. Stay tuned!
